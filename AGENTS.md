# Exercise selection guidelines

- Select worthwhile exercises first; assign muscle groups afterward.
- Prefer meaningful strength, mobility, stretching, balance, or coordination work.
- Reject trivial gestures, ineffective dance steps, placeholders, and filler.
- Use real named movements or postures; do not invent count-inflating variants.
- Draw broadly from fitness, rehabilitation, yoga, Pilates, dance, and martial arts.
- Keep all ground contact at the feet; exclude floor, seated, kneeling, and hand-supported work.
- Require zero equipment, including walls, chairs, partners, and props.
- Keep every movement practical both barefoot and in ordinary shoes.
- Keep every movement inside a 3 m × 3 m space.
- Keep movements quiet; exclude jumping, stomping, clapping, and vocalization.
- Exclude one-sided exercises; use symmetric, bilateral, or naturally alternating movements.
- Assign every exercise to all reasonably relevant muscle groups; full-body exercises are welcome.
- Assign by where the exercise is hardest, using practical best effort rather than exact anatomy.
- Maintain at least 10 quality exercises in every muscle group.
- Favor compound hand-loaded, isometric, coordination, and martial-arts hand work over trivial finger motions.
- Require an accurate human demonstration for every exercise; exclude synthetic or approximate media.
- Make hold demonstrations settle and remain on the final position during the timer.
