# Workout scope and selection

This is the current Android and web planner contract. It replaces the old
half-region eligibility, per-context quotas, category population floors and
percentage materiality requirements. The owner authorized this replacement on
2026-09-11. Historical quota reports remain historical evidence, not release gates.

## What a workout promises

Each block trains its demonstrated primary target. A shoulder movement can fill
an upper-body slot; it does not claim to train the entire upper body. Secondary
training claims contribute to workload accounting, never to slot eligibility.
Atomic sequences own the union of their members' primary slots and always run
complete, including both sides and directions. Aliases share a movement identity.

The selected duration is filled exactly with complete 45-second blocks and the
existing rest periods. Longer sessions may repeat complete sequences. Duration
does not promise a different movement in every minute or every muscle in every
setting. The requested grouping remains preferred; unavailable targets are
omitted, and broader groupings may be used when the finer plan cannot fit complete
sequences. At the broadest level only seven body-region subsets are considered.

Before starting a narrower plan, show its available regions, omitted targets and
actual scope. The user can adjust setup or explicitly start that limited plan.
When no complete plan fits, starting is blocked and setup remains editable.
Preparation and cancellation do not count as training or change feedback.
An older plan resumes only when its primary placements and completed work can be
preserved. Otherwise its actual blocks and feedback stay in history, the plan is
marked interrupted, and setup review is required. During an active workout a
changed setup must respect every unfinished block; if locked completed sequences
prevent a legal continuation, the change is refused without changing the workout.

## Hard boundaries and preferences

Silence, Insect, Hard Floor (rigid **and** slippery), Shy, clothing and declared
wall/mirror requirements apply to every member of every selected sequence.
Unreviewed compatibility never counts as permission. Light permits only sequences
whose every distinct member has demand 0; demanding work is never a fallback.
The existing automatic cadence, completion tolerance, recovery windows and native
Oura evidence gate retain their meaning. Recovery-derived Light remains a display
of recovery, not a persisted Light session or new selection predicate.

Within these boundaries, selection respects phase scores, valid Keeps, recovery,
equipment preferences and rotation among primary targets across logged workouts.
The existing muscle-load balancing acts across complete scheduled movements.
No muscle-specific bonus or extra secondary association may manufacture coverage.

## Catalog and release gates

Every canonical muscle retains genuine primary work somewhere in the approved
catalog. Admission still requires established copyable movements, exact reviewed
human demonstrations, ordinary shoes, at most 2 m × 2 m and feet-only floor contact,
with the existing narrow anatomical-media exception. Mirror and wall claims are
reviewed semantic claims, never category population targets. Controlled calf
raises retain the owner's Hard Floor-compatible/Insect-incompatible decision;
Heel Walks 194 remain Hard Floor-incompatible.

Release checks enforce metadata, reviewed media hashes, truthful primary coverage,
modifier behavior, strict sequence eligibility, exact timing, limited-scope consent,
state preservation, and Android/web parity. Adding legitimate quiet movements must
never create a new requirement to add noisy ones. Missing context-specific targets
are recorded in the current availability report as catalog improvements, with no
claim that a retired quota was filled by an exercise. Both platform tests, the web
production build and rendered user-flow verification remain required for release.
